India Free Live lists publicly available free-to-air streams already circulating openly. The app does not host the broadcasts, and no income is targeted from watching the channels. Rights belong to their respective broadcasters. Favorites and the chosen language stay on the device. Ads and purchases, when enabled, are provided by Google (AdMob and Play Billing).
